## Not every supplier makes the cut

When a product shows up in our catalog, it has already survived a scoring system most shops never bother with. Every supplier we work with - CJ, AliExpress, or Amazon-sourced - gets a live **health score from 0 to 100**.

### What we measure

- **On-time rate**: did the order ship inside the promised window?
- **Fulfillment rate**: how many orders get accepted, not rejected?
- **Defect rate**: how often do returns say "damaged" or "wrong item"?
- **Ship speed**: average days from order to carrier handoff.
- **Sync freshness**: hours since their stock feed last updated.

## What happens when a supplier slips

Scores under 60 stop receiving orders from us automatically. No meeting, no memo - the routing engine simply stops sending them work until they recover. You never see any of this; you just keep getting orders on time.

## Why this matters to you

A dropshipping store is only as good as its worst supplier. Most stores hide that. We'd rather build the boring plumbing that makes the worst case rare.
