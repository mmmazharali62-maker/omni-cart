## Everyone discounts. Not everyone tells the truth.

"Was $99, now $49!" Only works if it was ever actually $99. Here's how to check before Black Friday.

### 1. Check the 30-day history

Real stores keep price histories. Ours is public - every product page shows the **30-day range** and flags genuine drops of 5% or more. If a store won't show you history, assume the "original" price is decoration.

### 2. Watch the window, not the countdown

Countdown timers create panic; windows create deals. Our Black Friday sale runs **Nov 24-30 on both sides of the Atlantic** - no midnight surprises, no "ended early".

### 3. Free shipping beats 10% off on small orders

A $5 shipping fee quietly eats a 10% discount on a $40 basket. At Omni Cart, **$50+ ships free** in the US and **£40+** in the UK, always - Black Friday included.

### 4. Returns don't pause for sales

A doorbuster you can't return is a gamble, not a deal. Our **30-day window applies to everything**, including Black Friday orders.

## Our commitment this year

- No inflated "original" prices, ever.
- Sale prices visible on the 30-day chart.
- Stock counts shown honestly - when it's gone, it's gone.

*Happy hunting. The real deals are the ones that still look good in December.*
