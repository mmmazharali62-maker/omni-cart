## Straight answers on UK delivery

### Standard shipping

**7-14 business days** for mainland addresses, **9-18** for the Highlands and Islands. Express cuts that to **3-7 business days** (a bit longer up north).

### What it costs

| Zone | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| UK Mainland | £4.99 | £12.99 |
| Highlands & Islands | £8.99 | £19.99 |

Orders over **£40 ship free** on standard delivery.

## The £135 customs rule, explained properly

Under UK rules, orders **up to £135** are handled by the seller - you pay VAT at checkout and **nothing on delivery**. Over £135, the order ships DDU: UK Customs may collect duty when the parcel lands.

We don't hide this. If your basket crosses the line, checkout shows a plain-English warning before you pay.
